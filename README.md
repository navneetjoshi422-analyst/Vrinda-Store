# Vrinda-Store
This dashboard is ideal for e-commerce managers, marketing analysts, and business stakeholders to make data-driven decisions and improve sales strategy, inventory planning, and customer segmentation.
# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Sales Dashboard
## This interactive Excel dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the performance of an online fashion retail store, Vrinda Store. The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders quickly understand sales trends, customer demographics, order statuses, and top-performing categories, states, and channels.
## 🔍 Key Insights & Visuals:
- 📦 1. Highest Selling Category
- Visual: Line & column chart.
- Insight: The "Set" category is the highest-selling with 11,766 units, followed by Kurta (9,451) and Western Dress (3,590).
- Categories like Bottom (65) and Ethnic Dress (245) have the lowest sales.
## 🌍 2. Sales of Top 5 States
- Visual: Horizontal bar chart.
- Top-performing states:
- Maharashtra – ₹2.99M
- Karnataka – ₹2.65M
- Uttar Pradesh – ₹2.10M
- Telangana – ₹1.71M
- Tamil Nadu – ₹1.68M
## 📈 3. Orders vs Sales (Monthly)
- Visual: Combo chart with bars (sales amount) and lines (order count).
- Consistent performance across months with a peak in May and February.
- Highest order volume coincides with higher sales figures.
## 💰 4. Max Sales by Channel
- Visual: Column chart.
- Sales distribution across platforms:
- Amazon leads with ₹6.97M
- Followed by Myntra (₹4.62M) and Flipkart (₹4.25M)
- Lowest sales via Others (₹0.81M) and Meesho (₹0.87M)
## 🚚 5. Order Status
- Visual: Donut chart.
- Status breakdown:
- Delivered: 92%
- Cancelled: 3%
- Returned: 3%
- Refunded: 2%
## 👩‍🦰👨 6. Sales: Men vs Women
- Visual: Pie chart.
- Customer gender split:
- Women: 64% of total sales
- Men: 36%
## 👵🧒 7. Age vs Gender
- Visual: Clustered bar chart.
- Adults dominate in both male and female demographics.
- Adult Women: 34.59%
- Adult Men: 15.47%
- Teenage Women: 21.13%
- Senior Women: 13.7%
- Minor contribution from Teenage and Senior Men
## 📅 8. Slicers (Filters)
- Filters are available for:
- Month
- Category
- Channel
